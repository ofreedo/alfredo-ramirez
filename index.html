arconsultingwebsites.com/process

Nothing goes live until you say so.

  1. 01

    Talk

    We go over your business and goals. I review what you have now: your site, Google listing, and social pages.

  2. 02

    Build

    I design and write the new site on a private preview. Your current site stays up, untouched.

  3. 03

    Review

    You try the preview on your phone and computer and tell me what to change, as many rounds as it takes.

  4. 04

    Launch & grow

    The new site goes live on your domain, connected to Google. Then we keep improving it with SEO, ads, and updates.

arconsultingwebsites.com/faq

Before you email me.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business that needs customers to find it and trust it online. So far that includes a café, a paletería, a salon and spa, a painting contractor, and a general contractor.

What exactly do you do?

Web design and development, graphic design, copywriting, SEO, Google Ads, and ongoing website care. You can hire me for one piece or all of it.

My current site gets traffic. How do you avoid breaking it?

The new site is built on a private preview while your current site stays live. We switch over only when you've approved everything, and old links are pointed to the right new pages.

How long does a new website take?

A small business site usually takes a few weeks. Bigger sites with many pages or a store take longer. You'll get a timeline before we start.

What do you need from me?

A conversation at the start, plus whatever you have: photos, menu or services, prices, hours. After that, mostly your feedback on the preview.
