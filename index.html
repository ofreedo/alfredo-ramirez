arconsultingwebsites.com/faq

Before you email me.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any business that needs customers to find it and trust it online. So far that includes a café, a paletería, a salon and spa, a painting contractor, a general contractor, and a lodge.

What exactly do you do?

Web design and development, graphic design, copywriting, SEO, Google Ads, ongoing website care, and booking systems for lodging. You can hire me for one piece or all of it.

How does a project work?

We start with a conversation about your business, and I look over what you have now: your site, Google listing, and social pages. I build the new site on a private preview while your current site stays up. You try it on your phone and computer and tell me what to change. When you approve, it goes live on your domain, connected to Google, with your old links pointed to the right new pages.

How long does a new website take?

A small business site usually takes a few weeks. Bigger sites with many pages or a store take longer. You'll get a timeline before we start.

What do you need from me?

A conversation at the start, plus whatever you have: photos, menu or services, prices, hours. After that, mostly your feedback on the preview.

Is A.R. Consulting your full-time job?

No. I also work in tech as a Test Lead in Quality Engineering, and I run A.R. Consulting alongside it, so I take on a limited number of projects at a time. You always work with me directly.
