01 · About

Hi, I'm Alfredo.

I'm Alfredo Ramirez, and A.R. Consulting is my business. I help businesses build their websites.

Running a business leaves little time for a website. Maybe the menu online is out of date, the site is hard to use on a phone, or there's no site at all. That's where I come in: I gather what you already have (your services, menu, hours, and photos) and turn it into a clean, fast site on your own domain.

A.R. Consulting is a one-person business, so you work with me directly, from the first conversation through launch.

04 · How it works

Four steps.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Tell me about your business and what you want the site to do. I'll look over what you have now: your current site, Google listing, and social pages.

  2. 02

    Build

    I design and build the site from your real details: services or menu, hours, and photos.

  3. 03

    Review

    You try it on your phone and computer before anyone else sees it, and tell me what to change.

  4. 04

    Launch

    The site goes live on your domain, connected to Google, and I walk you through it.
