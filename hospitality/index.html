Before you switch.

Do I have to stop using Airbnb or Booking.com?

No. You keep every listing. Cloudbeds connects them to one calendar, so they stop competing for the same nights.

Can guests book directly on my website?

Yes. Cloudbeds includes a booking page you can link from your website, the way Masood's Lodge does, so direct guests book into the same calendar.

What happens to reservations I already have?

They move into Cloudbeds as part of the switch, so upcoming stays stay on the calendar.
